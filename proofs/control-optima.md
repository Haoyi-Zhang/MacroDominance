# Closed-form optima for the frozen synthetic controls

These arguments are independent of the dynamic-program tables and of the MILP
implementation. Coordinates below are those generated by `src/instances.py` and
`src/dominance_instances.py`. Nets occupy disjoint horizontal bands, so their
weighted HPWL contributions add.

## Masked family

For net `e_i`, the left and right fixed owner pins have horizontal coordinates 2
and `q+22`, while the variable pin lies at one of `11,...,10+q`. The fixed pins
therefore bracket every variable choice. All three pins have the same vertical
coordinate. Every assignment contributes exactly `q+20`, independently of the
variable candidate. With `k` unit-weight nets,

    W* = k(q+20).

Choosing candidate zero for every owner attains the bound.

## Exposed family

For net `e_i`, the inside pin is at horizontal coordinate `40i+4+c`, where
`c in {0,...,q-1}`. The outside pin chooses `40i+1` or `40i+34`; their vertical
coordinates differ by 20. Whichever inside choice is made, selecting the nearer
outside extreme yields minimum horizontal span 3: candidate zero pairs with the
left pin, and the rightmost inside candidate is still farther than 3 from the
right pin unless `q` changes beyond the frozen set. The frozen `q in {2,4}`
therefore has per-net minimum `3+20=23`, and independent outside choices attain
all minima simultaneously. Thus

    W* = 23k.

The paired hierarchy changes only the evaluation order, not this optimum.

## Biased family

For variable `i`, the weight-two local net `b_i` joins a fixed pin at `40i+1` to
a moving pin at `40i+4+c`, so its weighted horizontal span is `2(3+c)` and is
minimized at `c=0` with value 6. The unit-weight live net `e_i` joins that moving
pin to an outside pin at `40i+1` or `40i+34`; the vertical span is always 20.
At `c=0`, the left outside choice gives horizontal span 3, hence contribution 23.
For any `c>0`, the local term increases by `2c`, while the best possible reduction
of the live horizontal span is at most `c`; therefore no later candidate can
improve the sum. The per-variable minimum is `6+23=29`, independently attainable
for every `i`, and

    W* = 29k.

## Executable check

`tests/control_oracle.py` recognizes only the frozen family names, checks the
candidate-count inventory, evaluates an explicit attaining assignment using an
independent integer HPWL routine, and returns the formula value. The 77-case MILP
test compares these 26 formulas against a separate one-hot MILP. The formulas are
written arguments plus finite implementation checks, not proof-assistant output.
